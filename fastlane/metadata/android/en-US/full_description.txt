VNS Vault is a fully offline password manager for Android.

It stores your passwords, payment cards, identity documents and TOTP codes securely on your device using AES-GCM encryption. The app has no internet permission and never sends any data outside your device.

Features:
• Fully offline (no internet permission)
• Strong encryption (AES-GCM + PBKDF2)
• Biometric unlock (Fingerprint / Face)
• TOTP / 2FA authenticator
• Payment cards storage
• ID & Passport documents storage
• Android Autofill support
• Credential Provider support
• Multi-language support

Your data stays only on your device.
